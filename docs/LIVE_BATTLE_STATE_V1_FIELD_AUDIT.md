## Evidence boundaries

- `VERIFIED` means a matching STS1 bytecode declaration/reflection source exists; RNG also has prior real no-advance sampling. This does not mean the production protocol mapping is wired end to end.
- `UNVERIFIED` means the protocol/C++ target exists, but source conversion or complete diff is not verified.
- `UNAVAILABLE` means the collector has no trusted source or cannot recover the semantics; protocol input must be rejected.
- The enriched synthetic fixture populates hand/draw/discard/exhaust, representative player/monster statuses, relic counter and potion slots. Limbo/stasis are intentionally empty and nonempty values are rejected. This remains fixture-only, not a live comparison.
